# Scientific quality gates

Scientific claims cannot be approved by a scalar AI-generated quality score. A failing hard criterion blocks a release even when all other dimensions are excellent.

## G0: Authorization and provenance
- Research ownership, collaborators and approvals documented.
- Data and copyrighted materials legally available; research restrictions honored.

## G1: Literature integrity
- Search dates, search strings, database coverage and decisions recorded.
- Every material citation checked against original text and an exact locator.
- Retractions/corrections and meaningful counter-evidence considered.

## G2: Methodological integrity
- Research questions, estimands, units of observation, exclusions and hypotheses explicit.
- Instrumentation and measurement limitations disclosed.
- Dependence, multiplicity, missingness, model selection and construct validity scrutinized.

## G3: Computation and prediction
- Full chain from input hash to output table/figure reproducible.
- Diagnostics, uncertainty and sensitivity analyses pass independent review.
- For prediction claims, test unseen participants/interfaces under relevant splitting design; report baseline-relative, uncertainty-bounded metrics.

## G4: Scientific argument
- Every inference warranted; alternative explanations and null results addressed.
- Theoretical contribution is differentiated from existing work.

## G5: External fit and review
- Journal/solicitation instructions and AI-use policy verified within the permitted time window.
- Independent qualified reviewer/adjudicator consulted for consequential results.
- All relevant coauthors approve submission.

## G6: Release
- Approved manuscript/output and supplementary package versioned.
- No restricted materials inadvertently published.
- Submissions and public access only after PI approval.

The current validators check **structure**, not source truth or peer-review quality.
