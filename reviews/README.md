# Evidence synthesis, systematic review and meta-analysis

## Required workflow
1. Register or finalize protocol as appropriate, with review question, outcomes, inclusion and synthesis rules.
2. Design multi-database searches; save exact strings, interfaces, dates and retrieval counts.
3. Deduplicate and screen with documented independent human decisions where needed; AI may prioritize but must be validated.
4. Double-check study outcome extraction, unit conversions, effect estimates and dependency.
5. Choose a risk-of-bias instrument suitable to the design, not automatically RoB 2 for all.
6. Specify synthesis: narrative, random-effects, multilevel, robust variance, moderators and sensitivity, as justified.
7. Evaluate heterogeneity, imprecision, reporting/publication bias and certainty appropriately.
8. Produce PRISMA-aligned report with search log and reproducible meta-analysis.

No promise of feasible pooled meta-analysis until studies, comparability and effect-data availability are inspected.
