# Grant intelligence and proposal engineering

Grant workflows are investigator-led and sponsor-compliant.

- Discover solicitation, sponsor priorities, previously funded awards, reviewer criteria, eligibility, deadlines and current rules.
- Map research gaps to demonstrated sponsor needs; quantify novelty and practical relevance.
- PI constructs specific aims, central scientific hypotheses, originality, team and approach.
- Analyze feasibility, pilot evidence, statistical precision, recruitment, milestones, budget and alternative approaches.
- Conduct documented panel-style critique, conflict screening, ethics/data management and institution eligibility checks.
- Block mass-generated substantive application text when funder AI policies prohibit it; verify all language requirements before use.

**Current status:** templates only. No grant awards, opportunities, or reviewer scores have been verified.
