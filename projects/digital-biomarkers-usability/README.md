# Digital Biomarkers of Usability — publication project

**Scientific stage:** forensic inventory and independent analysis pending.

Author's 2025 UCF dissertation: [Digital Biomarkers of Usability](https://stars.library.ucf.edu/etd2024/445/). Source is already publicly hosted by UCF; the PDF is referenced, not duplicated in this temporary public repository.

## Proposed publication
Combine Experiments 2 and 3 in an empirical article on behavioral and oculomotor predictors of subjective usability. Use Experiment 1 principally for the theoretical basis; do not exclude or split other prior publications without an overlap analysis.

## Priority objectives
1. Confirm research rights, any prior overlapping publications, collaborative authorship and institutional ethics limits.
2. Reconstruct design and outcome-level data units for each study.
3. Reproduce every original primary inference from the original analysis code and raw/derived data.
4. Audit recovered cursor tracking and inferred-click proxies in Experiment 2.
5. Test whether fixation count contributes meaningful incremental validity beyond task duration for Experiment 3, accounting for clustered data.
6. Quantify out-of-sample prediction using grouping appropriate to the target generalization.
7. Independently assess theory, inferential limits and rival mechanisms.
8. Compare publication venues using actual recent articles, editorial rules and article-type constraints.
9. Obtain independent expert review and coauthor approval before submission.
