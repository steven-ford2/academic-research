# Proposed Experiments 2–3 journal article plan

**Working question:** Which passive behavioral measures show robust association and generalizable predictive validity for subjective task- and system-level usability ratings?

## Contribution to establish
- Appropriate benchmarks against time on task and simple baseline variables.
- Contrasting mouse versus eye metrics under controlled usability differences.
- Matched tests across websites and outcomes, with defensible measurement interpretation.
- Clarified boundaries on task, participant, site, and domain transfer.
- Reproducible supplementary analytical package.

## Investigator-led argument structure
1. Practical problem and exact theoretical gap, verified against current sources.
2. Relevant theory and prior comparisons, including contradictory evidence.
3. Research questions and estimands.
4. Experiment 2 methods, instrumentation recovery and robustness.
5. Experiment 3 methods, visual behavior, sites/tasks and measurements.
6. Replicated results including uncertainty, null findings and model comparison.
7. Independent predictive tests and sensitivity analyses where methodologically justified.
8. Interpretation, alternative explanations, practical use, measurement limitations, and future validation.
